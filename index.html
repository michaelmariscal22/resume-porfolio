About

I spent nearly four years as an IT Instructor at Cebu Eastern College, teaching courses from Data Structures and Algorithms to Database Systems and Web Technologies. My approach centers on hands-on, project-based learning—students write real code, build working applications, and learn debugging and system design through practice.

Today, I build database-driven web applications and continue expanding my development toolkit. I'm looking for opportunities where I can contribute both technical skills and the ability to communicate complex ideas clearly—whether that's in a development role, a technical education position, or a hybrid of both.
